# Governance

Ludus currently has a single maintainer, who makes the final call on scope, design and
releases. Being upfront about that is more useful than implying a committee that does not
exist.

What it means in practice:

- **Decisions are explained, not just made.** Anything structural gets a short decision record
  in [docs/architecture/adr/](docs/architecture/adr/), including the options that were
  rejected and why. You should be able to disagree with a decision on its merits without
  having to guess at the reasoning.
- **Issues and pull requests get an answer.** Possibly "no" — but with a reason, and not after
  six months of silence.
- **Roadmap changes are visible.** The roadmap lives in the README and moves in public.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no committee to join yet. Someone who contributes substantially and consistently, and
who reviews other people's work well, will be invited to become a maintainer, and this document
will be rewritten to describe how decisions get made between more than one person. That is a
good problem to have.

## If this project is abandoned

If there has been no maintainer response for six months, treat the project as unmaintained and
fork it. The licence exists so that you can. Nobody needs permission.
